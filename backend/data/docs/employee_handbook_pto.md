# Employee Handbook — Leave & PTO (Acme Corp)

## Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue **20 days** of PTO annually, credited monthly.
Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rata basis.

### Carryover
A maximum of **5 days** may be carried into the next calendar year. Unused PTO above
the carryover limit is forfeited unless prohibited by local law.

### Request Process
- Submit PTO requests through Workday.
- Requests for absences longer than **3 consecutive days** must be submitted at least
  **10 business days** in advance.
- Manager approval is required; conflicts are resolved by business need and seniority.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 sick days per year, separate from PTO. Doctor documentation may
be requested for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave
Eligible employees receive 12 weeks of parental leave for birth or adoption.
Coordinate with HR at least 30 days before the expected leave start date when foreseeable.

## Holidays
Acme Corp observes 11 paid company holidays annually. The holiday calendar is published
each November for the following year.
