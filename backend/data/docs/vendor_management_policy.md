# Vendor Management Policy — Acme Corp

## 1. Purpose
Ensure third-party vendors meet Acme security, privacy, and operational standards
before processing company or customer data.

## 2. Vendor Risk Tiers
- **Tier 1**: Processes Restricted or personal data, or has production network access.
- **Tier 2**: Processes Confidential data without personal data.
- **Tier 3**: No access to Confidential/Restricted data (e.g., office supplies).

## 3. Onboarding Requirements
- Tier 1 and Tier 2 vendors must complete a security questionnaire.
- Any vendor processing confidential or personal data requires a signed
  **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** before onboarding.
- Contracts above **$50,000** require Legal review prior to signature.

## 4. Continuous Monitoring
Tier 1 vendors are reassessed annually. Material changes in data processing scope
trigger an immediate re-assessment.

## 5. Offboarding
Upon contract termination, vendors must certify destruction or return of Acme data
within 30 days, with evidence retained by Procurement.
